# Visual reports — iteration 2

Not written yet, and deliberately so. A "visual report" — several artifacts gathered into one
structured comment, harvested from a run and composed rather than attached one call at a time —
depends on the `harvest`, `compose` and `report` commands, none of which exist in this iteration.

What exists today is single-call delivery: [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) covers deciding whether to attach
at all (§1), capturing safely (§2–§5), reviewing before sending (§4) and handling refusals (§7–§10).

Until iteration 2 lands, a multi-artifact point is made with one `attach` call carrying several
files and one caption — §1 still decides how many artifacts that should be, and the answer is
usually one.
